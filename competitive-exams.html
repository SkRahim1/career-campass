Important Notice for Telangana Students (MeeSeva & ePASS Certificates)

Most state government exams (TS POLYCET, TG APRJC CET, TSWREIS Inter CET) and subsequent ePASS fee reimbursement schemes require an active MeeSeva Caste Certificate and Income Certificate issued in the current financial year. Ensure you prepare these early to avoid last-minute application rush.
