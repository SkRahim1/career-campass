TG EAPCET

Telangana Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test (TG EAPCET 2026)

Conducted by: Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU) Hyderabad on behalf of TGCHE

TG EAPCET (formerly TS EAMCET) is Telangana's premier state-level entrance exam for admissions into professional undergraduate courses: B.Tech (Engineering), B.Pharmacy, Pharma.D, B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture, B.V.Sc (Veterinary), and B.F.Sc across JNTUH, Osmania University, and 200+ top engineering colleges.

Apply on Official PortalOfficial Notification PDF
Application DeadlineApril 10, 2026 (Without Late Fee)
Exam DateMay 7 - May 11, 2026 (Multiple CBT Sessions)
Registration Fee₹900 for General/BC (₹500 for SC/ST/PH) per stream
Exam ModeOnline (CBT)
Duration & Marks160 Marks (160 Multiple Choice Questions)

Official Eligibility Requirements

Educational Qualification

Passed or appeared in Intermediate (10+2) examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) or Biology, Physics, and Chemistry (BiPC) conducted by TSBIE or recognized board.

Age Limitations & Relaxations

Candidate should have completed 16 years of age as on 31st December. No upper age limit for Engineering.

Minimum Marks / GPA Required

Minimum 45% marks (40% in case of candidates belonging to reserved category) in the specified group subjects at Intermediate level.

Domicile & Local Status (Telangana)

Candidates must satisfy the Local / Non-Local status requirements of Telangana State.

Mandatory Subjects: Intermediate MPC (for Engineering) or BiPC (for Agriculture & Pharmacy).
