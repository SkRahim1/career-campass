TSWREIS Inter CET

Telangana Social & Tribal Welfare Residential Junior Colleges Entrance (TSWREIS / TTWREIS CET 2026)

Conducted by: Telangana Social & Tribal Welfare Residential Educational Institutions Society, Hyderabad

TSWREIS & TTWREIS conduct this state-level entrance test for admissions into Class 11 (Intermediate 1st Year) in Centres of Excellence (COEs) and General Social/Tribal Welfare Residential Junior Colleges across Telangana. Provides 100% free residential education, uniforms, bedding, textbooks, and premier coaching for IIT-JEE, NEET, CLAT, and CA Foundation.

Apply on Official PortalOfficial Notification PDF

Official Eligibility Requirements

Educational Qualification

Students who appeared / passed SSC (10th) from any recognized school in Telangana in regular stream.

Age Limitations & Relaxations

Candidate should not exceed 17 years of age as of 31st August.

Minimum Marks / GPA Required

Pass in SSC examination with minimum GPA of 5.0 for SC/ST and 6.0 for other categories.

Domicile & Local Status (Telangana)

Candidate must be a bonafide resident of Telangana state.

Mandatory Subjects: Must have studied Class 10 in English or Telugu medium.
