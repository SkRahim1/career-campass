Message from the Co-Founder
“Every year, thousands of talented Class 10 students pick the wrong stream simply because of peer pressure or lack of awareness. At Excellence Voices and Career365Compass, our aim is crystal clear: to walk beside every student, break down complex career paths into simple steps, and give them the confidence to build a future they truly love.”

As the co-founder of Excellence Voices and Director at Career365Compass, Rahim Shaikh has been actively involved in youth development, student outreach, and grassroots educational drives. Seeing the confusion students and parents face right after the 10th board exams, he helped shape Career365Compass into a practical, localized guidance platform.

Whether a student wants to prepare for government residential colleges, crack POLYCET, explore high-paying IT careers without a conventional degree, or secure 100% fee reimbursement via government scholarships, Rahim and the mentoring team ensure no question goes unanswered.

How We Help Students

From the day 10th exam ends to your first day in college, we guide you through every choice.

Stream & Course Selection

Comprehensive roadmaps for Intermediate (MPC, BiPC, MEC, CEC, HEC), Polytechnic Diplomas, ITI trades, and Vocational courses tailored to individual strengths.

College Discovery & Cutoffs

Verified database of 50+ Government, Residential, and Private Junior Colleges in Hyderabad and Telangana with fee breakdowns, facilities, and contact details.

Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement

Clear, step-by-step navigation for ePASS, TS residential welfare schemes, pre/post-matric scholarships, and minority educational grants.

Entrance Exam Preparedness

In-depth guidance on POLYCET, TG APRJC CET, Gurukulam, and residential entrance examinations, eligibility rules, and prep strategies.

25-Question Diagnostic Survey

A groundbreaking student survey that pinpoints personal interests, family budget constraints, and post-10th confusions to provide instant college matches.

One-on-One Mentorship

Direct, compassionate counseling where students and parents can speak directly to mentors to address their deepest fears about life after 10th.
