Investment Banker

Investment Bankers facilitate corporate finance transactions, including helping companies raise capital via IPO shares, managing corporate mergers (M&A), and structuring bond issues.

Starting Salary scale

₹9.0 Lakhs - ₹40.0 Lakhs

Annual average scale in India

Min Qualification

MBA (Finance) from Top Tier B-Schools / CA / CFA

Key Skills

Corporate FinanceValuation ModelingNegotiationAnalytical ThinkingExcel & PowerPointStamina

Future Scope

Highly lucrative career. Deals in mergers and corporate expansion are growing in emerging markets like India.

Key Responsibilities

  • •Build detailed corporate financial projection sheets
  • •Draft investment decks and corporate sales pitch books
  • •Coordinate legal due-diligence in corporate acquisitions
  • •Structure IPO pricing strategies

Educational Milestones Roadmap

10

Milestones Immediately After Class 10

1. Choose Intermediate MEC or CEC stream. 2. Focus on corporate business reports and economic policies.
12

Milestones After Intermediate (11th & 12th) / Diploma

1. Complete B.Com, BBA, or B.Tech. 2. Complete MBA Finance from premier institutes (like IIMs) or clear CA.
