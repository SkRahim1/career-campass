Available Scholarships & Fee Concessions

Telangana State ePASS Fee Reimbursement

Full or partial tuition fee reimbursement provided by TS Government for eligible SC, ST, BC, and Minority candidates.

Eligibility: Annual parental income under ₹2 Lakhs (Urban) / ₹1.5 Lakhs (Rural).

Merit Academic Fee Waiver

Institution provides fee concessions for high achievers securing 9.5+ GPA in SSC Board exams.

Eligibility: SSC Grade 9.5+ or CBSE 90%+
